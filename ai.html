Our stance on Generative Artificial Intelligence

The use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (Gen AI) is a controversial topic, especially within the coding/development space.

With recent improvements within the last 1-3 years of Generative AI models, AI is now able to generate things such as Images, Code, Entire Websites and more.

As a result from Generative Artificial Intelligence Models becoming more advanced, it raises
a lot of ethical questions such as copyright infringement, job displacement, the environmental impact and much more.

We at ray-y.co.uk are against using Generative AI

The use of GenAI within our work

We stand strongly against using GenAI and we aim to give you reassurance that our services are made by humans and that
Generative AI was not involved within the creation or development of any of our services or sites.

We believe that generative artificial intelligence should not be replacing the jobs of developers, artists, or anyone in general.
