Our stance on Generative Artificial Intelligence

Generative Artificial Intelligence (Gen AI) is a huge topic, especially within the coding/development space.

With recent improvements within the last 1-3 years of Generative AI models, AI is now able to create lots of things from apps, to games, and more.

When you're using ray-y.co.uk or any of its accociated sites or projects, we'd like you to know that generative AI is not involved with the creation or any other part.

We believe that artificial intelligence should not be replacing the jobs of developers, artists, or anyone.
